    Good to know before we start.

    How do payment and VAT invoicing work?

    We settle through Useme. With “Sign a contract”, you deposit funds with Useme before work starts; the platform releases them after acceptance under its terms. Useme issues the VAT invoice.

    When will the project be ready?

    I agree the deadline after reviewing the scope, materials and access needed. Larger projects have separate deadlines for each stage.

    How many revisions are included?

    We record the revision rounds and acceptance criteria before starting. New features outside that scope need a separate quote.

    Do I need to send you my password?

    We arrange access through an invitation or a separate account with the necessary permissions. Do not send your main account password in a message.

    What about code ownership and licences?

    We agree copyright transfer or licensing terms in the contract and Useme settlement. Third-party themes, plugins and libraries retain their own licences.

    What do I receive at the end?

    The agreed deliverable and an “Opis dzieła” PDF. File and access handover, along with any ongoing support, are defined in the project scope.
